Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how GILNET Admin Hub handles information when authorized administrators use the Hub's company invitation feature.

Information processed

The Hub processes administrator account and access information needed to provide the service. For company invitations, it stores recipient email addresses, invitation subjects and message text, delivery status, timestamps, and message identifiers. When an invitation receives a reply, the Hub can store the reply's sender and recipient addresses, subject, plain-text body, message identifiers, and timestamp.

How Gmail access is used

The Hub uses Google Gmail API access to send company invitations and replies from support.gilnet.property.manager@gmail.com and to retrieve replies for invitation threads already tracked by the Hub. Its Gmail permissions support sending mail and reading those threads. The Hub does not intentionally search or import unrelated mailbox messages. OAuth credentials are held as server-side Supabase Edge Function secrets; access tokens are used by the server, not embedded in the Android or desktop app.

Storage and access

Invitation records and tracked replies are stored in the GILNET Supabase project. Access through the Hub is restricted to authorized Super Admins. Records remain in the project until they are removed through the project's administration or retention processes; the Hub does not currently apply an automatic deletion schedule to invitation records.

Service providers

Google provides the Gmail API used to send and retrieve tracked email threads. Supabase provides authentication, server functions, and database hosting for the Hub. Their handling of information is also subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

The support mailbox owner can revoke the Hub's Gmail access in their Google Account. Revoking access will stop invitation sending and reply synchronization until the integration is authorized again. For questions or requests concerning Hub records, contact the GILNET support team.
